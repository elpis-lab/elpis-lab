MQPs

Major Qualifying Projects advised by the ELPIS Lab, the capstone project of WPI undergraduates.

2025–2026

Robotic Solution for Automated Loading of Centrifuges

An affordable way to automate plate loading in benchtop centrifuges

A robotic mechanism that works with a SCARA arm to load and unload microplates in a conventional benchtop centrifuge.

  • Students: Grace Amlicke, Tejas Balcha, Nick Palumbo
  • Advisors: Prof. Constantinos Chamzas, Prof. Mohammad Mahdi Agheli Hajiabadi
  • Sponsor: Aera Therapeutics

Toward Automated Parachute Packing

Robotic Line Stowing

A multi-arm robotic system that stows parachute suspension lines into elastic loops with vision-guided control.

  • Students: Chenhan Guan, Zheren Li, Fiona Prendergast, Gunnar Rorapaugh, Oliver Van Campen
  • Advisors: Prof. Constantinos Chamzas, Prof. Connor McCann, Prof. Mohammad Mahdi Agheli Hajiabadi, Prof. Jing Xiao
  • Sponsor: U.S. Army Natick Soldier Systems Center

2024–2025

GRACE

General Robot Assistant for Common Errands

A mobile manipulator that navigates unknown environments, finds a target object, picks it up, and places it at a semantic goal location.

  • Students: Brent Weiffenbach, Alexander Beck, Gwenaelle DeLeo
  • Advisors: Prof. Jing Xiao, Prof. Constantinos Chamzas, Prof. Reza Ebadi
