## Overview

Centrifugation is a critical step in many experimental assays, from sample preparation to analysis. Benchtop centrifuges are inexpensive (about $1k) but require a scientist to manually load plates, wait for the spin, and unload them. Automated centrifuges integrate into automated labs, but cost around $60,000 per unit, which is a barrier for many organizations.

This MQP developed a robotic system that collaborates with an existing SCARA-type arm, such as the [PreciseFlex 400](https://www.brooks.com/laboratory-automation/collaborative-robots/preciseflex-400/), to automate the loading and unloading of microplates in a conventional benchtop centrifuge.

The arm can accurately pick and place microplates in a horizontal orientation, but most centrifuges require vertical insertion or deep placement into the rotor, which the arm alone cannot do. The project designed an additional robotic mechanism that works with the arm to load and unload plates into the rotor with precision and reliability.
