ELPIS Lab was [awarded](https://www.wpi.edu/news/announcements/robotics-professor-constantinos-chamzas-awarded-175k-nsf-grant-advance-robot-learning) the [NSF-CRII grant](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2451108&HistoricalAwards=false) for 175k$ to conduct research on how robots can learn abstract, symbolic representations from visual and execution data.
